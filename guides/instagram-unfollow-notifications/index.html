Does Instagram Notify Someone When You Unfollow Them?

Updated November 20, 2026

Short answer: no. Instagram doesn’t send any notification when you unfollow someone, and it doesn’t show up in their activity. They can only find out by looking.

What the other person can still notice

There’s no alert, but an unfollow isn’t invisible either. Someone who pays attention can spot it in a few ways:

  • Their follower count drops by one. Few people notice a single follower, but accounts with small followings sometimes do.
  • You’re missing from their Followers list. If they search for your name there, you won’t appear.
  • Your Following list no longer includes them. If your account is public, or they follow you, they can open your Following list and look.
  • Unfollower-tracking apps. Some people use third-party apps that compare their followers over time and flag who left.

Unfollowing a private account

If you unfollow a private account, you lose access to its posts and stories right away. To see them again you have to send a new follow request, and that request does notify them. So if you unfollow a private account by accident, they will only find out if you ask to follow again.

Removing a follower is silent too

Instagram also lets you remove someone from your own followers. That doesn’t notify them either. They simply stop seeing posts from you if your account is private, and they won’t be in your Followers list anymore.

Unfollowing a lot of people at once

Instagram limits how many follows and unfollows an account can make in a short time. If you unfollow dozens of accounts in a row you may see an “Action blocked” or “Try again later” message. It’s temporary; spreading unfollows out over a few days avoids it.

Tip: before a cleanup, find out who actually doesn’t follow you back so you don’t unfollow people who do.
