How to See Who Unfollowed You on Instagram

Updated November 20, 2026

Instagram doesn’t keep a list of who unfollowed you and doesn’t notify you when it happens. You can still find out by checking one person, by comparing your followers with who you follow, or by saving your followers list and comparing it later.

1. Check one person

If you’re wondering about someone specific, open your own profile, tap Followers and search for their username. If they don’t show up, they unfollowed you, removed you, or never followed you in the first place.

2. See everyone who doesn’t follow you back

To check everyone at once, use the data export Instagram gives you. It contains your full followers and following lists, and you don’t need to give any app your password.

  1. In Instagram, open the menu (☰) and tap Accounts Center.
  2. Tap Your information and permissions, then Export your information, then Create export.
  3. Choose your profile and Export to device.
  4. Under Customize information, select only Followers and following.
  5. Set Date range to All time. Any shorter range leaves people out.
  6. Choose JSON (HTML works too) and tap Start export. Instagram emails you when the ZIP is ready, usually within minutes.

Then drop the ZIP onto Follower Analyzer. It compares the two lists in your browser and shows who you follow that doesn’t follow you back, who follows you that you don’t follow back, and your mutuals. Nothing is uploaded.

3. Track unfollowers over time

An export is a snapshot of one moment. To see exactly who left between two dates:

  1. Analyze an export today and use Export as CSV to save your lists.
  2. A few weeks later, request a new export and analyze it again.
  3. Anyone who was in your followers before but isn’t now has unfollowed you (or deactivated their account).

Avoid apps that ask for your password

Many “unfollowers” apps ask you to log in with your Instagram account. That gives a stranger full access to your account, and logins from automated apps can get accounts flagged or locked. Your official data export gives you the same information without that risk.

Numbers don’t match? Make sure the export’s date range was All time. Deactivated accounts can also appear in an export but not in the app.
