How it works

Three steps, about five minutes

  1. Request your export

    In Instagram's Accounts Center, export only Followers and following.

    Set date range to All time

  2. Drop the ZIP here

    Instagram emails you when it's ready. Drop the ZIP as-is; there's nothing to unzip.

  3. See your lists

    Who doesn't follow you back, who you don't follow back, and mutuals. Sort, search and export them.

Step-by-step: export your data from Instagram
1Open Instagram and tap the menu (☰) in the top right
2Tap Accounts Center
3Tap Your information and permissions
4Tap Export your information, then Create export
5Select your profile and choose Export to device
6 Tap Customize export and set three things:
Information: tap "Customize information", clear all, then select Connections > Followers and following only
Date range: set to All time. This is critical, or your counts will be incomplete
Format: choose JSON or HTML. Both work
7Tap Start export and wait for Instagram to process it (usually a few minutes, sometimes longer)
8Download the ZIP when it's ready and drop it above. You can also unzip it and drop just the followers and following files
What we look for: files under connections/followers_and_following/ in the ZIP (nested folders are fine). We load every followers_1, followers_2, … and following file, in JSON or HTML. Files dropped on their own are matched by name.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I see who doesn't follow me back on Instagram?
Export your followers and following data from Instagram (Account Center, then Your Information and Permissions, then Export your information) with the date range set to All time. Drop the ZIP, or the followers and following files, onto this page. You'll get three lists: people who don't follow you back, people you don't follow back, and mutuals.
Can I see who unfollowed me on Instagram?
Yes, by comparing two exports. Analyze today's export, then open the Unfollowed you tab and add one you downloaded earlier: you'll see who unfollowed you and who started following you in between. Instagram doesn't keep that list itself, so if you don't have an older export yet, keep today's for next time.
Does Instagram notify anyone when I check?
No. You're only downloading a copy of your own data. Follower Analyzer never logs in to your account and never contacts the people on your lists.
Is it safe? Do I need to give my password?
You never enter your Instagram password here. Your files are read and compared in your browser and are never uploaded to a server.
Can using this get my Instagram account banned?
No. Follower Analyzer never logs in to Instagram or acts on your account; it only reads the data export Instagram itself gives you. Accounts get flagged by apps that ask for your password or unfollow people automatically, and this does neither.
What information leaves my browser?
Nothing from your export. The files, and the lists built from them, are never uploaded or sent anywhere. The only thing on this page that uses the network is Google Analytics, which counts visits and which steps and buttons get used, such as finishing an analysis, exporting a list or clicking Buy me a coffee. It sees the usual visit details (browser, device, rough location) and nothing from your export: no usernames, no file names and no list sizes. Nothing is saved in your browser either, unless you tick Remember this analysis.
Why don't my numbers match the Instagram app?
Usually because of inactive accounts. Instagram's export still lists accounts that were deactivated or deleted, but your profile doesn't count them, so the export's "You follow" number is often higher than the app's. On the Not Following You Back tab, enter the Following number from your profile to see roughly how many are inactive. If the export is missing people instead, its date range probably wasn't set to All time.
Should I export as JSON or HTML?
Either works. Follower Analyzer reads both formats, and files are sorted automatically by name.
Does it work for business and creator accounts?
Yes. Business and creator accounts export followers and following from Accounts Center the same way, and the files work exactly like a personal account's.
Is Follower Analyzer free?
Yes, free and unlimited. Check as often as you like, with no account, no app to install, no payment and no limit on how many times you run it. If it helped you, you can buy me a coffee, but you never have to.
