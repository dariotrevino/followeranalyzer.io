How to export your Instagram data
1Open Instagram and tap the hamburger menu (☰) in the top right
2Tap Account Center
3Tap Your Information and Permissions
4Tap Export your information, then Create Export
5Select your profile and choose Export to Device
6 Tap Customize export and configure three settings:
Information: tap "Customize information", clear all, then select Connections > Followers and Following only
Date range: set to All time — this is critical, or your counts will be incomplete
Format: choose either JSON or HTML — this app supports both
7Tap Start Export and wait for Instagram to process it (usually a few minutes, sometimes longer)
8Download the ZIP when it's ready — you can drop the ZIP here as-is, or unzip and drop the followers / following files
What we look for: Inside the ZIP, files under connections/followers_and_following/ (nested folder prefixes are fine). We load all followers_1, followers_2, … and following in JSON or HTML. You can also drop those files directly — filenames are auto-routed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see who doesn't follow me back on Instagram?
Export your followers and following data from Instagram (Account Center, then Your Information and Permissions, then Export your information) with the date range set to All time. Drop the ZIP, or the followers and following files, onto this page. You'll get three lists: people who don't follow you back, people you don't follow back, and mutuals.
Does Instagram notify anyone when I check?
No. You're only downloading a copy of your own data. Follower Analyzer never logs in to your account and never contacts the people on your lists.
Is it safe? Do I need to give my password?
You never enter your Instagram password here. Your files are read and compared in your browser and are never uploaded to a server.
What information leaves my browser?
Your export files, and the lists built from them, are never uploaded. Two things do use the network. First, the page loads Google Analytics to count visits and which steps and buttons get used, such as finishing an analysis, exporting a list or clicking Buy me a coffee. It sees the usual visit details (browser, device, rough location) and nothing from your export: no usernames, no file names and no list sizes. Second, if you press Check accounts, the usernames on your Not Following You Back list are sent to a public proxy (allorigins.win) so they can be looked up on Instagram. That only happens when you press the button. Nothing is saved in your browser either, unless you tick Remember this analysis.
Why don't my numbers match the Instagram app?
Usually the export's date range wasn't set to All time, so older follows are missing. Deactivated or deleted accounts can also appear in your export but not in the app. The account lookup on the Not Following You Back tab can help you spot them.
Should I export as JSON or HTML?
Either works. Follower Analyzer reads both formats, and files are sorted automatically by name.
Is Follower Analyzer free?
Yes. There's no account, no app to install and no payment. If it helped you, you can buy me a coffee, but you never have to.
